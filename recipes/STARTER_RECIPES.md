# Starter recipes

Six original v0.1 examples written for Chef Bob. They are vegetarian and have explicit quantities so you can try planning and serving-size changes. They have not been kitchen-tested. Cooking times are estimates. Check ingredient labels against your own allergies and restrictions.

Each recipe makes 2 servings. Multiply ingredient quantities by `desired servings / 2`. Scale water used in a sauce or to cook rice by that ratio; water used only for boiling pasta or noodles needs enough room for the food. Cooking time and pan size do not scale by the same ratio. Keep any rounding or package-size adjustments visible in the grocery list.

These six different dishes can fill two weeks of three meals each without repeats. The order below follows `RRNRRN`, where R means rice and N means noodles or pasta. This is an optional example, not a dietary recommendation.

### Tomato chickpea rice

**ID:** `starter-tomato-chickpea-rice`

**Servings:** 2

**Time:** About 10 minutes active; 40 minutes total.

**Review:** Original v0.1 example, not kitchen-tested.

#### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| White rice, dry | 150 | g |
| Chickpeas, cooked and drained | 240 | g |
| Chopped canned tomatoes | 400 | g |
| Onion, diced | 100 | g |
| Garlic, minced | 6 | g |
| Olive oil | 15 | ml |
| Water | 200 | ml |
| Salt | 2 | g |
| Ground black pepper | 0.5 | g |

#### Method

1. Rinse the rice and drain it. Put a medium saucepan with a lid over medium heat.
2. Add the oil and onion. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the rice, chickpeas, tomatoes, water, salt, and pepper. Stir and bring to a gentle boil.
4. Cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook for 18 to 20 minutes. Check that the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed. If it is still firm and dry, add a little water and continue cooking; record any adjustment if saving the recipe.
5. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes. Fluff the rice and divide between two bowls.

#### Notes

- The quantity of chickpeas is their weight after draining, not the can's total weight.
- Rice brands absorb different amounts of liquid. The times and liquid ratio need kitchen testing.

### Lemon white bean rice

**ID:** `starter-lemon-white-bean-rice`

**Servings:** 2

**Time:** About 10 minutes active; 30 minutes total.

**Review:** Original v0.1 example, not kitchen-tested.

#### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| White rice, dry | 150 | g |
| Water | 300 | ml |
| White beans, cooked and drained | 240 | g |
| Spinach, washed | 120 | g |
| Garlic, minced | 6 | g |
| Olive oil | 15 | ml |
| Lemon juice | 20 | ml |
| Salt | 2 | g |
| Ground black pepper | 0.5 | g |

#### Method

1. Rinse and drain the rice. Put it in a saucepan with the water. Bring to a boil, cover, and cook over low heat for 12 to 15 minutes, until tender. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes.
2. While the rice rests, heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add the drained beans and cook for 3 minutes, stirring. Add the spinach and cook for about 2 minutes, until wilted and the beans are hot throughout.
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Divide the rice between two bowls and spoon the beans and spinach on top.

#### Notes

- Use cooked beans. The times do not include cooking dried beans.
- If the rice package requires a different water ratio or cooking time, use that instruction and record the change.

### Ginger tofu noodles

**ID:** `starter-ginger-tofu-noodles`

**Servings:** 2

**Time:** About 20 minutes active; 25 minutes total.

**Review:** Original v0.1 example, not kitchen-tested.

#### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Wheat noodles, dry | 180 | g |
| Firm tofu, drained and cubed | 250 | g |
| Broccoli, small florets | 200 | g |
| Carrot, thinly sliced | 100 | g |
| Ginger, grated | 10 | g |
| Garlic, minced | 6 | g |
| Neutral cooking oil | 15 | ml |
| Soy sauce | 30 | ml |
| Water for sauce | 60 | ml |
| Water for boiling noodles | 2000 | ml |

#### Method

1. Bring the noodle water to a boil. Cook the noodles according to their package, then drain. The total time assumes noodles that cook in 4 to 6 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the tofu and cook for 6 minutes, turning it so several sides become lightly golden.
3. Add the carrot, broccoli, ginger, garlic, and the 60 ml sauce water. Cover and cook for 4 minutes, until the vegetables are tender enough to eat.
4. Uncover, add the soy sauce and drained noodles, and toss for 2 minutes until hot. Divide between two bowls.

#### Notes

- Contains soy and wheat. A substitute needs its own ingredient and label check.
- The boiling water is drained; it is not part of the sauce.

### Lentil carrot rice

**ID:** `starter-lentil-carrot-rice`

**Servings:** 2

**Time:** About 15 minutes active; 30 minutes total.

**Review:** Original v0.1 example, not kitchen-tested.

#### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| White rice, dry | 150 | g |
| Water for rice | 300 | ml |
| Lentils, cooked and drained | 240 | g |
| Carrot, finely diced | 150 | g |
| Onion, diced | 100 | g |
| Tomato passata | 200 | g |
| Water for sauce | 100 | ml |
| Olive oil | 15 | ml |
| Ground cumin | 2 | g |
| Salt | 2 | g |

#### Method

1. Rinse and drain the rice. Put it in a saucepan with its 300 ml water. Bring to a boil, cover, and cook over low heat for 12 to 15 minutes, until tender. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a second saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 6 minutes, stirring.
3. Add the cumin and stir for 30 seconds. Add the passata, 100 ml sauce water, lentils, and salt.
4. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring once, until the carrot is tender and the lentils are hot. Divide the rice between two bowls and spoon the lentil mixture over it.

#### Notes

- Use cooked lentils. The times do not include cooking dried lentils.
- Finely diced carrot cooks faster than large chunks.

### Egg and pea fried rice

**ID:** `starter-egg-pea-fried-rice`

**Servings:** 2

**Time:** About 15 minutes active; 35 minutes total, including cooking the rice.

**Review:** Original v0.1 example, not kitchen-tested.

#### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| White rice, dry | 150 | g |
| Water | 300 | ml |
| Eggs | 2 | count |
| Frozen peas | 120 | g |
| Carrot, finely diced | 100 | g |
| Onion, diced | 60 | g |
| Neutral cooking oil | 15 | ml |
| Soy sauce | 15 | ml |

#### Method

1. Rinse and drain the rice. Cook it with the water in a covered saucepan over low heat for 12 to 15 minutes after bringing it to a boil. Check that it is tender, then turn off the heat and let it rest for 5 minutes.
2. While the rice rests, heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
3. Add the frozen peas and cook for 3 minutes. Push the vegetables to one side. Beat the eggs, pour them into the empty side, and stir for about 2 minutes until fully set.
4. Add the warm cooked rice and soy sauce. Stir-fry for 3 minutes until everything is hot, then divide between two bowls.

#### Notes

- Contains egg and soy; many soy sauces also contain wheat. Check the actual product label.
- This version uses freshly cooked rice. It does not require storing cooked rice overnight.

### Tomato lentil pasta

**ID:** `starter-tomato-lentil-pasta`

**Servings:** 2

**Time:** About 15 minutes active; 30 minutes total.

**Review:** Original v0.1 example, not kitchen-tested.

#### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Wheat pasta, dry | 180 | g |
| Lentils, cooked and drained | 240 | g |
| Chopped canned tomatoes | 400 | g |
| Onion, diced | 100 | g |
| Garlic, minced | 6 | g |
| Olive oil | 15 | ml |
| Dried oregano | 2 | g |
| Salt | 2 | g |
| Water for boiling pasta | 2000 | ml |

#### Method

1. Bring the pasta water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to its package, then drain. The total time assumes pasta that cooks in 10 to 12 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, stirring. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, lentils, oregano, and salt. Simmer uncovered for 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens slightly and the lentils are hot.
4. Add the drained pasta to the sauce, toss well, and divide between two bowls.

#### Notes

- Contains wheat. Check the pasta label for other ingredients that matter to your household.
- Use cooked lentils. The times do not include cooking dried lentils. The pasta boiling water is drained.
